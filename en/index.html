04 — The place

At the end of the road, right at the top

You climb the corniche, cross the state forest, and the road simply stops: 187 metres above the Mediterranean. An Iberian setting, and a view that takes in the beaches, the pines and the horizon in one sweep.

The view
The beaches under the pines, the Thau lagoon behind, and the sun dropping straight into the sea.
The table
“La mesa” is Spanish for the table: plates made for the middle of it, shared without ceremony.
