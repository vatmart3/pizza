Home/Restaurant with a sea view
Sète · Mont Saint-Clair

Eating facing the sea, without being at the water’s edge

In Sète, “sea view” means two very different things. A table on the harbour, with the water two metres away. Or a table 187 metres above it, where you see the sea, the beaches, the lagoon and the town all at once. La Mesa is the second.

01 — The view

What you actually see

No superlatives: here is what is in front of you when you sit down.

In front

The sea and the beaches

The Mediterranean from one end of the horizon to the other, and the long ribbon of beach running west under the pines. From up there, you see the curve.

Behind

The Thau lagoon

On the other side of the summit, the lagoon and its oyster tables, flat as a plate. Sète is an island between the two — this is where you understand that.

Around

The Pierres Blanches forest

Pines, scrubland and white limestone — the rock the place is named after. You eat in a state forest, not on a seafront car park.

02 — When to come up

The same view four times a day

That is the advantage of a house open from 8.30 am until nightfall: the view is never the same, and you can choose which one you want.

Morning
The sun is behind you, the sea is hard and bright, the air is still cold. This is when you see the furthest.
Midday
The light falls straight down, the water turns white. You eat in the shade of the reed canopy, and look out less.
Golden hour
The last hour before sunset. Everything turns orange, the stone included. It is the most requested service, and you need to book.
Night
Sète lights up below, the lanterns take over. April to September only.

Tonight’s sunset time

03 — On the plate

A view is not enough to make a meal

That is the classic complaint about panoramic restaurants, and it is often deserved. Here the slate is written every morning from the market and the catch — that is the only promise, and it holds every single day.

04 — Practical

What to know before coming up

  • You drive up the corniche, and parking is free on site — which, in Sète in summer, is not a detail.
  • Without a car, bus line 5 comes up to the Les Pierres Blanches stop, a few metres away.
  • Booking by telephone on +33 4 67 53 33 40. We confirm every table by voice.
  • Step-free access to the dining room and the main terrace, adapted toilets.
  • Annual closing from December to March. Reopening on 1 April.
